I'm John Heisler, a Communication and Collaboration Administrator with nearly 10 years in IT. I manage about 2,000 users across two hybrid Microsoft 365 tenants, and I write a lot of PowerShell to keep that manageable.

This blog is where I share the scripts and concepts that come out of that work:

- Bulk changes and repetitive tasks in Entra ID, Active Directory, Exchange, SharePoint, and Teams
- Data gathering and reporting
- Troubleshooting mail flow, calling, and permissions
- Lessons from email and tenant migrations to Microsoft 365

Every script is also on [GitHub](https://github.com/Snowstuff123). Test anything in a lab before running it in production.
